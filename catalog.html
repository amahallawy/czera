FAQ

Egyptian cotton towels, explained.

What makes Czera towels different?

We use long-fibre Egyptian cotton from the Nile Delta and weave at hospitality densities (600–800 GSM). The fibre length lets a denser terry hold its softness; the GSM gives the towel its weight in the hand and its absorbency on the skin.

Each piece is hand-finished in Egypt with a rolled selvedge and woven Czera tag.

Which weight (GSM) should I choose?

600 GSM is light, dries fastest, and works well for hand and face towels. 700 GSM is our most-chosen everyday bath weight. 800 GSM is the spa weight: heavier in hand, more plush, longer to dry.

How do I care for an Egyptian cotton towel?

Machine wash warm with similar colours. Tumble dry low. Skip fabric softener — it coats the fibre and reduces absorbency. Iron on low if you prefer.

Czera towels reach their softest after the first three washes.

Do you ship outside Egypt?

Yes — by quote. Message us on WhatsApp with the items and your delivery address and we’ll come back with shipping options and an arrival window.

Do you offer hospitality (bulk) pricing?

Yes. We supply hotels, spas, and short-let properties with custom sizes, colour matching, and embroidery. Reach out on WhatsApp and we’ll send you the hospitality programme.
