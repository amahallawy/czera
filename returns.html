Returns & Exchanges Policy

At CZERA, your satisfaction matters to us. Every product is carefully prepared and checked to ensure it meets our quality standards.

1. Returns & Exchanges

Customers may request a return or exchange within 14 days of receiving their order, subject to applicable Egyptian consumer protection laws and the conditions outlined below.

Products must be returned in their original condition, unused, unwashed, and with their original packaging and tags, where applicable.

2. Hygiene & Personal Care Products

For hygiene and quality reasons, towels, bathrobes and other personal-use textile products that have been used, washed or damaged after delivery cannot be returned or exchanged simply due to a change of mind, except where required by applicable law.

3. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery and share clear photos of the issue.

Our team will review your request and arrange an appropriate solution, including replacement, repair or refund, as applicable under Egyptian consumer protection law.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed through the appropriate payment method, in accordance with applicable laws and payment provider procedures.

5. How to Request a Return or Exchange

To submit a request, please contact our customer service team and provide:

  • Your order number.
  • The reason for your request.
  • Photos of the product, if applicable.

Our team will guide you through the process.
