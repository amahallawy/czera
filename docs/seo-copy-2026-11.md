## FAQ

For the new Questions page at `/faq/`. Where the two sites order differently,
the answer has a **Store** and a **Design site** variant; otherwise one answer
serves both. All eleven questions in the spec can be answered from the
confirmed facts, so none is left out.

### What are CZERA towels made of?

100% cotton. That is true of every CZERA towel and bathrobe, including the
beach towels and every piece in the gift sets.

### How heavy are they (GSM)?

The towels in the bath range, the face towel, hand towel, bath towel and bath
sheet, are 650+ GSM, and so are the towel gift sets. The bathrobes are
1200 GSM, including the robes in the gift sets.

### What sizes are there?

The face towel is 30 × 30 cm, the hand towel 50 × 100 cm, the bath towel
70 × 140 cm and the bath sheet 90 × 160 cm. The beach towels are
100 × 200 cm. The bathrobes have a generous fit for up to 130 kg.

### Who do the bathrobes fit?

The bathrobes have a generous fit for up to 130 kg. They come in white and in
black, and both are 100% cotton at 1200 GSM.

### What comes in a gift set?

Every set comes in the black CZERA gift box with satin ribbon, and each product
page lists what is in the box. The towel sets hold three to five towels from
the bath range. His & Hers Robes holds a white and a black bathrobe. The robe
and beach towel sets hold one bathrobe and one striped beach towel, and the
Luxury Beach Towel Gift Set holds two striped beach towels.

### Can I build my own box?

Yes. On the Build your box page you choose up to six towels and up to two
bathrobes, with at least two pieces in all, and they are packed together in one
CZERA box.

### How do I order and pay?

**Store**

Add the pieces you want to your cart and place the order at checkout. You pay
on delivery, in cash. After you order, the team calls you to confirm the order
and to tell you the delivery fee.

**Design site**

Add the pieces you want to your bag, fill in your delivery details and tap
"Send order by email". Your email app opens with the order already written:
tap Send there to place it. If your email app doesn't open, copy the order and
send it to support@czeramaison.com. The team replies by email to confirm
delivery and payment. You pay on delivery.

### Is there a delivery fee?

**Store**

Yes. The team tells you the delivery fee when they call to confirm your order.

**Design site**

Yes. The delivery fee is confirmed by email when the team replies to your
order.

### Can I return or exchange?

Yes, within 14 days of receiving your order, for items that are unused and
unwashed and in their original packaging. The Returns & exchanges page has the
full policy and how to make a request.

### Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes. Email support@czeramaison.com with what you need, and the team will reply.

### How do I contact you?

Email support@czeramaison.com. CZERA is also on Instagram, @czera_maison, and
on Facebook, CzeraMaison.
