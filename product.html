Care
Machine wash warm with similar colours. Tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softener — it coats the fibre and reduces absorbency. Iron on low if desired. The towel will reach its softest after the third wash.
Origin & Craft
Spun and woven in Egypt from Nile Delta long-fibre cotton. Hand-finished and packed in Cairo. Each piece carries an inspection number on the inner tag.
